## ✨ Features

- **Premium Dark UI** – Sleek, high-contrast design with a consistent color palette.
- **Fully Responsive** – Optimised for mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- **Scroll Reveal Animations** – Subtle entrance effects for sections and cards.
- **Interactive Navigation** – Fixed navbar with smooth scrolling and mobile hamburger menu.
- **Hero Section** – Name, tagline, call-to-action buttons, and resume download.
- **About Me** – Professional summary with key highlights and metrics.
- **Achievements Grid** – Icons and impact numbers (ICSO, GPA, outreach).
- **Project Cards** – Hover effects, tech tags, GitHub & live demo links.
- **Experience Timeline** – Clean vertical timeline for leadership and work.
- **Blog/Articles Section** – Placeholder cards for future writing.
- **Contact Form** – Client‑side validation with success feedback.
- **Resume Download** – One‑click `.txt` resume generation (easy to replace with PDF).
- **SEO Optimised** – Semantic HTML, meta tags, and structured headings.
- **Fast & Lightweight** – Pure HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript – no bloated frameworks.
